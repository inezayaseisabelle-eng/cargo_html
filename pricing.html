FAQ

Pricing questions

When does the "Outside Kigali" rate apply?

Whenever your destination is outside the City of Kigali — for example Musanze, Rubavu, Huye, Nyungwe or Akagera. Standard cars go from RWF 30,000 to RWF 50,000 per day, and Premium cars from RWF 50,000 to RWF 70,000 per day.

Is fuel included?

No. Vehicles are delivered with a full tank and should be returned full. Electric vehicles are delivered fully charged.

Do I need a driving licence?

Yes. You need a valid driving licence and a National ID or passport. International visitors can use an international driving permit.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. You can cancel any upcoming booking from your account page free of charge.
